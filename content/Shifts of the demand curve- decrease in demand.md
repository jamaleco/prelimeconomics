[[Shifts of the demand curve]]
- consumers are less willing to pay for as much of a [[product]] at a given [[price]] point (as opposed to buying 2 coffees for $3.9 each theyll only buy one)
- [[consumers]] are also less likely to pay as much for a product at a given quantity point. (if they used to spend 3.9 dollars for 1 coffee theyre only prepared to pay 3 dollars now)
