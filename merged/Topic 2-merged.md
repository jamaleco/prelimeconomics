# How External Economies of scale work

how [[External economies of scale]] work
- increased localisation of [[industry]]. Stanford providing silicon valley [[firm]]s with technologically capable students and workers
- Growth of whole industry: investment into electricity to find alternative energy fuels after the middle east crisis
- better [[capital]] [[Market]] results in benefit to all- cheaper [[Investment]] funds from more people


# How internal diseconomies of scale don't work

[[Internal diseconomies of scale]]
- management/owner communication issues with day-to-day operations, resulting inefficiency 
- Large firm size may result in excess paperwork and duplicatioj
- complicated workplace relations
- in essence, managerial efficiency decreases resulting in organisational errors which can create company wide issues

# Impacts of technological development on economies

- [[price]]s are impacted by a more informed [[Market]] place through search engines, increasing [[Competition]] causing harsher [[profit]] margins

- [[employment]] is impacted by technological advancement, [[capital goods]] make many jobs redundant cutting prices drastically. increased [[Competition]] decreases local benefits and may result in greater overseas business operations, impacting local employment and thus the overall local [[economy]]

- stronger [[capital goods]] results in greater quality [[production]] with less [[cost]], in turn allowing them to address [[Market]] [[demand]] and thus, as their brand improves, they can make more [[profit]] and have increased [[demand]]. However as [[Investment]] is required for capital goods, short term loss will occur, potential at large magnitudes and the investment may not pay off- technology output may be weak, 

- [[product]] such as [[SAAS]] are possible due to new technology which also may generate more [[industry]]s and also greatly impact previous industries- cars, phones, computers etc. cheaper [[capital goods]] may make production easier as well, results in more potential products for mass [[Market]]s

- [[Ethical decision making]] also impacts [[product]] choices and [[production]]- ethical consumerism or organic foods can find a high [[demand]] [[Market]], creating [[profit]] simply. Also impacts [[employment]]

- technology also globalises [[Market]]s- [[FDI]], international [[Investment]], [[imports]]/[[Exports]], cheaper [[Labour]] ([[Ethical decision making]] results in ensuring that poorer countries treat their workers better through pressure from more affluent countries) 

- Environmental sustainability-[[Ethical decision making]]

[[Internal economies of scale]]
